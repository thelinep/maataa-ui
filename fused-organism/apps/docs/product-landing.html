02 · Certified kernel

11 active packages. M2 is certified on pinned Chromium 144.0.7559.96; everything else is explicit roadmap.

The public surface mirrors architecture.json. Browser certification applies only to the published M2 surface matrix—not to all 90 headless registry entries or roadmap domains.
